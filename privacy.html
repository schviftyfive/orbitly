Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Orbitly LLC ("Orbitly," "we," or "us") collects when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Orbitly LLC is a company registered in Kentucky that builds apps for creators. You can reach us at contact@orbitly.llc.

Information we collect

Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, such as your name or your company.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you request. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

This website does not have user accounts. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on it. If that changes, we will update this page first.

How we use information

We use what we collect to respond to your messages, operate and secure this website, understand any technical problems, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Orbitly, such as our email provider (Google Workspace) and our website hosting provider, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights, our users, or others.

Our products

This policy covers the Orbitly website only. Our products, including Starside, have their own privacy policies that describe how information is handled inside those apps.

How long we keep information

We keep emails and related records for as long as we need them to respond to you, run the business, and meet legal requirements. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email contact@orbitly.llc and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor them.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Write to us at contact@orbitly.llc.
